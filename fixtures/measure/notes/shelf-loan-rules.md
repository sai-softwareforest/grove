# Loan rules, as agreed with the office

- A loan runs for 21 days.
- A member holds at most three books.
- The fine is 20 pence for each day a book is late and stops at the price
  of the book.
- The day the book was due is free.
- A member with an overdue book cannot borrow.

The office manager can waive a fine; the software does not know about
that.
